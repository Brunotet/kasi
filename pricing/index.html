Questions people ask

How long does it take?

Starter and Business sites go live in 7 days. Ecommerce sites take 2 to 4 weeks.

How do I pay?

You pay 50% to start and 50% when the site is finished.

What do I need to send you?

Send your business name, what you sell or do, your logo and your photos on WhatsApp. We build the pages from that.

What is the difference between Starter and Business?

Starter is a finished website with no monthly fee, ideal when your content rarely changes. Business adds SEO, monthly edits, a monthly blog post and support, to help you grow on Google.

Will my site rank on Google?

Nobody can promise page one. Every site is built with the technical foundations Google needs. On the Business plan we keep improving them every month, and we publish new content that people search for.

How do shop orders and payments work?

On the Ecommerce plan, customers pay through a payment link (Yoco or PayFast) or send their order on WhatsApp.
