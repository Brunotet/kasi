Website design for caterers

Someone planning a function wants to see your food, your packages and whether you are free on their date. A website that answers those three questions gets you the quote request.

What your customers search for

Customers search for the event and the place: wedding catering, funeral catering, corporate lunch, birthday party food, with a town or area. Then they compare photos, menus and how fast you reply.

What your website should show

  • Photos of real dishes and real events, not stock pictures.
  • Your packages by event type: weddings, funerals, corporate functions, birthdays.
  • A sample menu, with "from" prices per person if you can give them.
  • The areas you serve and how far ahead people should book.
  • Minimum and maximum guest numbers you handle.
  • A WhatsApp button for quote requests.

Make WhatsApp do the work

A quote needs the date, the number of guests, the venue and the type of event. A button that opens a message with those blanks means the first message you get already has what you need.

Example message: Hi, I need catering for [type of event] on [date] for about [number] guests in [area].

Pages you need

Home, Menus and packages, Gallery, About and Contact. That is 5 pages, which fits the Starter plan.

Which plan fits

Starter (from R1 499) suits caterers with set packages. Choose Business (R899 once off, then R499 a month) to publish seasonal menus and blog posts like "how to plan funeral catering" that answer searches. See all plans and limits.

Questions people ask

Should I publish my prices?

Give "from" prices per person or per package. Event costs vary, but a starting price filters out quotes that will not work and saves you time.

Can I take orders on the site?

Quote requests go through the WhatsApp button. If you also sell trays or food boxes, the Ecommerce plan lets customers pay by payment link.

How do I show I am reliable?

Show photos of real events, say how far ahead people should book, and add real client reviews to your Google Business Profile.

Do I need a page for each type of event?

On the Business plan you can have separate pages for weddings, funerals and corporate catering. Each one can match a more specific search.
